Hello, call me Bia!

I'm a Brazilian BIM and structural technician based in County Galway, Ireland. When I'm not deep in modelling, you'll find me hiking, hunting down good vegan street food, doting on my cat, or exploring new countries. I have ADHD, which means I bring high energy, deep curiosity, and genuine passion to every problem I take on.

My path began in Brazil, studying civil engineering and working on site and in construction management. Moving to Ireland opened new possibilities: I completed an MSc in Global BIM Management and committed to one mission — bringing practical digital construction workflows to the teams and projects that need them most. For me, BIM is ultimately about people, process, and metadata working together to deliver smarter and more sustainable projects.
